PARAMETER: Triglycerides

WHAT IT IS:
Triglycerides are a type of fat (lipid) found in blood, used for energy storage.

PURPOSE OF THE TEST:
Measured as part of a lipid panel to assess cardiovascular and metabolic health.

GENERAL REFERENCE RANGE (fasting, adults, approximate):
Below 150 mg/dL is generally considered normal.

WHAT LOW VALUES CAN SOMETIMES INDICATE:
Low triglycerides are uncommon and can sometimes be associated with malnutrition or certain thyroid conditions.

WHAT HIGH VALUES CAN SOMETIMES INDICATE:
High triglycerides can sometimes be associated with diet high in sugar/fat, obesity, alcohol use, diabetes, or certain medications.

IMPORTANT CAVEATS:
Reference ranges vary between laboratories and individuals. Triglyceride levels can be affected by recent food intake, so fasting is often required before testing.
