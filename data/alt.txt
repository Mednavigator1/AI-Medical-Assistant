PARAMETER: ALT (Alanine Aminotransferase)

WHAT IT IS:
ALT is an enzyme found mainly in liver cells that helps with protein metabolism.

PURPOSE OF THE TEST:
Measured to assess liver health and detect liver cell damage.

GENERAL REFERENCE RANGE (adults, approximate):
7 to 56 U/L

WHAT LOW VALUES CAN SOMETIMES INDICATE:
Low ALT is generally not a concern and is usually considered normal.

WHAT HIGH VALUES CAN SOMETIMES INDICATE:
High ALT can sometimes be associated with liver inflammation, fatty liver, viral hepatitis, certain medications, or alcohol use.

IMPORTANT CAVEATS:
Reference ranges vary between laboratories and individuals. ALT is often interpreted together with AST and other liver markers.
