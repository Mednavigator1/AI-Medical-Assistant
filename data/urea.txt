PARAMETER: Urea (Blood Urea Nitrogen / BUN)

WHAT IT IS:
Urea is a waste product formed when the body breaks down protein, filtered out by the kidneys.

PURPOSE OF THE TEST:
Measured to assess kidney function and hydration status.

GENERAL REFERENCE RANGE (adults, approximate):
7 to 20 mg/dL

WHAT LOW VALUES CAN SOMETIMES INDICATE:
Low urea can sometimes be associated with a low-protein diet, liver disease, or overhydration.

WHAT HIGH VALUES CAN SOMETIMES INDICATE:
High urea can sometimes be associated with reduced kidney function, dehydration, high-protein diet, or gastrointestinal bleeding.

IMPORTANT CAVEATS:
Reference ranges vary between laboratories and individuals. Urea is often interpreted together with creatinine for kidney function assessment.
