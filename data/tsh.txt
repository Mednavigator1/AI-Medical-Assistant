PARAMETER: TSH (Thyroid-Stimulating Hormone)

WHAT IT IS:
TSH is a hormone from the pituitary gland that signals the thyroid gland to produce thyroid hormones, which regulate metabolism.

PURPOSE OF THE TEST:
Measured to screen for and monitor thyroid function (hypothyroidism or hyperthyroidism).

GENERAL REFERENCE RANGE (adults, approximate):
0.4 to 4.0 mIU/L

WHAT LOW VALUES CAN SOMETIMES INDICATE:
Low TSH can sometimes be associated with an overactive thyroid (hyperthyroidism) or excess thyroid medication.

WHAT HIGH VALUES CAN SOMETIMES INDICATE:
High TSH can sometimes be associated with an underactive thyroid (hypothyroidism).

IMPORTANT CAVEATS:
Reference ranges vary between laboratories and individuals. TSH is often interpreted along with free T3/T4 levels for a fuller picture.
