PARAMETER: White Blood Cells (WBC)

WHAT IT IS:
White blood cells (leukocytes) are part of the immune system and help the body fight infections and diseases.

PURPOSE OF THE TEST:
Measured as part of a CBC to check for infection, inflammation, allergic reactions, or blood/bone marrow disorders.

GENERAL REFERENCE RANGE (adults, approximate):
4,000 to 11,000 cells/µL

WHAT LOW VALUES CAN SOMETIMES INDICATE:
Low WBC count can sometimes be associated with viral infections, bone marrow problems, autoimmune conditions, or certain medications.

WHAT HIGH VALUES CAN SOMETIMES INDICATE:
High WBC count can sometimes be associated with bacterial infection, inflammation, stress response, smoking, or certain blood disorders.

IMPORTANT CAVEATS:
Reference ranges vary between laboratories and individuals. WBC count alone does not identify the cause; a "differential" breakdown and clinical context are needed.
