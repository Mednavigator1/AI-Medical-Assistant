PARAMETER: Creatinine

WHAT IT IS:
Creatinine is a waste product from normal muscle breakdown, filtered out of the blood by the kidneys.

PURPOSE OF THE TEST:
Measured to assess kidney function.

GENERAL REFERENCE RANGE (adults, approximate):
- Men: 0.7 to 1.3 mg/dL
- Women: 0.6 to 1.1 mg/dL

WHAT LOW VALUES CAN SOMETIMES INDICATE:
Low creatinine is generally not concerning and can sometimes be associated with lower muscle mass.

WHAT HIGH VALUES CAN SOMETIMES INDICATE:
High creatinine can sometimes be associated with reduced kidney function, dehydration, or high muscle breakdown.

IMPORTANT CAVEATS:
Reference ranges vary between laboratories, muscle mass, age, and sex. Creatinine is often used with eGFR to assess kidney function more accurately.
