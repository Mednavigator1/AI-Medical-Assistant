PARAMETER: Total Cholesterol

WHAT IT IS:
Cholesterol is a fat-like substance used to build cells and hormones; it travels in the blood combined with proteins.

PURPOSE OF THE TEST:
Measured to assess cardiovascular (heart and blood vessel) risk as part of a lipid panel.

GENERAL REFERENCE RANGE (adults, approximate):
Below 200 mg/dL is generally considered desirable.

WHAT LOW VALUES CAN SOMETIMES INDICATE:
Very low cholesterol is uncommon but can sometimes be associated with malnutrition, liver disease, or certain genetic conditions.

WHAT HIGH VALUES CAN SOMETIMES INDICATE:
High total cholesterol can sometimes be associated with diet, genetics, sedentary lifestyle, or other metabolic conditions, and may relate to increased cardiovascular risk.

IMPORTANT CAVEATS:
Reference ranges vary between laboratories and individuals. Total cholesterol is usually interpreted together with HDL, LDL, and triglycerides, not alone.
