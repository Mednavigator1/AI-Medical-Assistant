PARAMETER: Glucose (Blood Sugar)

WHAT IT IS:
Glucose is the main sugar found in blood and is the body's primary energy source.

PURPOSE OF THE TEST:
Measured to screen for and monitor diabetes, prediabetes, or blood sugar regulation problems.

GENERAL REFERENCE RANGE (fasting, adults, approximate):
70 to 99 mg/dL (fasting); values differ for random or post-meal tests.

WHAT LOW VALUES CAN SOMETIMES INDICATE:
Low glucose (hypoglycemia) can sometimes be associated with excess insulin, skipped meals, certain medications, or hormonal issues.

WHAT HIGH VALUES CAN SOMETIMES INDICATE:
High glucose (hyperglycemia) can sometimes be associated with diabetes, prediabetes, stress, or certain medications.

IMPORTANT CAVEATS:
Reference ranges vary between laboratories, testing method (fasting vs random), and individuals. A single reading is not enough to diagnose diabetes.
