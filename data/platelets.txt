PARAMETER: Platelets (Thrombocytes)

WHAT IT IS:
Platelets are small cell fragments in the blood that help it clot and stop bleeding.

PURPOSE OF THE TEST:
Measured as part of a CBC to assess clotting ability and detect bleeding or clotting disorders.

GENERAL REFERENCE RANGE (adults, approximate):
150,000 to 450,000 /µL

WHAT LOW VALUES CAN SOMETIMES INDICATE:
Low platelet count (thrombocytopenia) can sometimes be associated with viral infections, autoimmune conditions, certain medications, or bone marrow problems.

WHAT HIGH VALUES CAN SOMETIMES INDICATE:
High platelet count (thrombocytosis) can sometimes be associated with inflammation, infection, iron deficiency, or bone marrow disorders.

IMPORTANT CAVEATS:
Reference ranges vary between laboratories and individuals. Extreme values (very high or very low) generally warrant prompt medical attention.
