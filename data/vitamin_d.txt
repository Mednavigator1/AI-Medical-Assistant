PARAMETER: Vitamin D (25-hydroxyvitamin D)

WHAT IT IS:
Vitamin D is a fat-soluble vitamin important for calcium absorption, bone health, and immune function.

PURPOSE OF THE TEST:
Measured to assess vitamin D sufficiency, especially in people with bone issues, limited sun exposure, or certain risk factors.

GENERAL REFERENCE RANGE (adults, approximate):
20 to 50 ng/mL is generally considered sufficient; below 20 ng/mL is often considered deficient.

WHAT LOW VALUES CAN SOMETIMES INDICATE:
Low vitamin D can sometimes be associated with limited sun exposure, dietary insufficiency, malabsorption, or certain medical conditions.

WHAT HIGH VALUES CAN SOMETIMES INDICATE:
High vitamin D is uncommon and can sometimes be associated with excessive supplementation.

IMPORTANT CAVEATS:
Reference ranges vary between laboratories and individuals, and recommendations differ by region and population.
