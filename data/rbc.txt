PARAMETER: Red Blood Cells (RBC)

WHAT IT IS:
Red blood cells (erythrocytes) carry oxygen throughout the body using hemoglobin.

PURPOSE OF THE TEST:
Measured as part of a CBC to evaluate oxygen-carrying capacity and detect anemia or other blood disorders.

GENERAL REFERENCE RANGE (adults, approximate):
- Men: 4.7 to 6.1 million cells/µL
- Women: 4.2 to 5.4 million cells/µL

WHAT LOW VALUES CAN SOMETIMES INDICATE:
Low RBC count can sometimes be associated with anemia, blood loss, nutritional deficiencies, or bone marrow disorders.

WHAT HIGH VALUES CAN SOMETIMES INDICATE:
High RBC count can sometimes be associated with dehydration, lung disease, high altitude, or certain bone marrow conditions.

IMPORTANT CAVEATS:
Reference ranges vary between laboratories and individuals. RBC count is usually interpreted together with hemoglobin and hematocrit.
