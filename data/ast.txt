PARAMETER: AST (Aspartate Aminotransferase)

WHAT IT IS:
AST is an enzyme found in the liver, heart, and muscles, released into the blood when these tissues are damaged.

PURPOSE OF THE TEST:
Measured to assess liver health, and sometimes muscle or heart conditions.

GENERAL REFERENCE RANGE (adults, approximate):
10 to 40 U/L

WHAT LOW VALUES CAN SOMETIMES INDICATE:
Low AST is generally not a concern and is usually considered normal.

WHAT HIGH VALUES CAN SOMETIMES INDICATE:
High AST can sometimes be associated with liver damage, muscle injury, heart conditions, or alcohol use.

IMPORTANT CAVEATS:
Reference ranges vary between laboratories and individuals. AST is less specific to the liver than ALT, so both are usually interpreted together.
